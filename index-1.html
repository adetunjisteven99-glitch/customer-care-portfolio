Demonstration work

Examples of customer conversations.

These are sample responses created to demonstrate my communication style, not real client testimonials or completed client projects.

Order inquiry

Customer asks about a delayed order

Customer: “My order hasn’t arrived yet. Can you help?”

Sample reply: “I’m sorry your order hasn’t arrived as expected. I can help check the status. Please share your order number, and I’ll review the available tracking information and explain the next steps.”
Complaint handling

Customer is unhappy with a product

Customer: “I’m disappointed with what I received.”

Sample reply: “I’m sorry the product didn’t meet your expectations. I’d like to understand what happened and help you review the options available under our return or exchange policy.”
Product question

Customer needs more information

Customer: “Is this item available in another size?”

Sample reply: “Thanks for your interest! I can help you check the available sizes. Could you tell me which size you’re looking for? I’ll guide you based on the current product information.”
